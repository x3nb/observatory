Concept Builder

Start with a clear, simple idea. Your concept is the core of everything.

  • Define the problem you want to solve.
  • Describe your idea in one sentence.
  • Identify who benefits from it.
  • Write a short concept brief (1–2 paragraphs).
  • Check feasibility: time, skills, resources.

Planning Steps

Turn your concept into a structured plan.

  • Create a timeline with key milestones.
  • List resources: tools, people, money, time.
  • Assess your skills and where you need support.
  • Identify risks and how to reduce them.
  • Set measurable goals for each phase.

Execution Workflow

Move from planning into consistent action.

  • Break the project into small, clear tasks.
  • Use daily or weekly work cycles.
  • Track progress in a simple log or board.
  • Review what’s working and adjust as needed.
  • Protect time for deep focus work.

Funding Strategies

Explore ways to support your project financially.

  • Self‑funding: savings, small budget, gradual growth.
  • Crowdfunding: share your story, build community support.
  • Grants: research programs aligned with your field.
  • Investors: prepare a clear pitch and value proposition.
  • Partnerships: collaborate with people or brands.

Completion Checklist

Guide your project from “almost done” to truly complete.

  • Quality check: test, review, refine.
  • Prepare launch materials: copy, visuals, messaging.
  • Plan your launch: date, platform, audience.
  • Gather feedback after launch.
  • Create a maintenance plan: updates, support, improvements.
